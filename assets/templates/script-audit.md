# Script audit

Result: `PASS / FAIL`

## Evidence gate

- [ ] Every material claim maps to an evidence-table ID.
- [ ] No invented dialogue, inner thought, motive, action, number, or composite chronology is presented as fact.
- [ ] Conflicts and uncertainty are expressed at the correct strength.
- [ ] Source apparatus stays backstage unless source identity is part of the story.

## Story gate

- [ ] The first line is universal enough to understand before names and dates.
- [ ] The opening contains a relationship, abnormal action or result, and a supportable judgment.
- [ ] The protagonist wants something and pays a concrete cost.
- [ ] Each scene changes what can happen next.
- [ ] Necessary facts become situation and movement, not a list of knowledge points.
- [ ] One evidence-led recognition change occurs.

## Spoken-language gate

- [ ] One breath and one main idea per sentence.
- [ ] Specialized terms are removed or immediately explained.
- [ ] Verbs and consequences are concrete and colloquial.
- [ ] Description creates cause, pressure, or cost rather than decoration.
- [ ] Names, fixed phrases, and quotations will not be split by narration.

## Ending gate

- [ ] The protagonist's account is settled before abstraction.
- [ ] Ordinary-person cost appears when relevant.
- [ ] One portable new understanding is stated plainly.
- [ ] Only one principal question remains.
- [ ] Any continuation hook follows the causal chain and is not an advertisement.

## Five-sentence retell

1. Who meets what abnormal situation?
2. What do they want to keep or gain?
3. What makes the problem worse?
4. What do they choose, and who pays?
5. What new understanding does the listener gain?

If any answer is unclear without looking at the script, result is `FAIL`.
