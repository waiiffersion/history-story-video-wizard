## Causal spine test

- [ ] Each scene changes the protagonist's situation.
- [ ] Adjacent scenes have a causal, chronological, contrast, escalation, or evidence relationship.
- [ ] Side stories pass all four branch-story gates.
- [ ] The episode is not padded to reach a target duration.
