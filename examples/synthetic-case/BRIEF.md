# Brief — fictional training material

Working title: Why did the new steward open an empty granary?

Universal pressure: How does a newly appointed leader prove loyalty when the previous leader left no supplies?

Audience's likely first explanation: the steward secretly gave grain away.

Desired revision: the available records support an empty storehouse and a later accusation, but do not establish theft, motive, or the identity of recipients.

Boundary: do not invent a speech, a crowd, a punishment, a precise grain amount, or a storm.
