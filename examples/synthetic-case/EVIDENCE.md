# Evidence — fictional training material

All sources and claims below are invented for workflow demonstration.

| ID | Planned claim | Source identity and date | Exact support | Conflict or alternative | Certainty class | Allowed narration | Forbidden overclaim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | The storehouse was nearly empty when inspected | fictional inventory tablet, near-contemporary | three empty-bin marks and one sealed-bin mark | marks do not identify who removed supplies | documented observation | “The inventory marks show more empty bins than sealed ones.” | “The steward emptied the granary.” |
| C02 | A later story accused the steward of giving grain away | fictional chronicle written generations later | explicit accusation | no recipient or amount is named | source claim | “A later chronicle blamed the steward.” | “The accusation is proven.” |
| C03 | The steward's authority was immediately contested | C01 plus C02 | empty storage and later blame are consistent with a legitimacy dispute | other explanations remain possible | supported inference | “The surviving versions place the steward under suspicion.” | invented motive, speech, arrest, or crowd reaction |

## Shared minimum

The material permits a story about uncertainty and blame. It does not permit a solved theft story.
